GOOD TO KNOW ✳

Before you
head out.

Clear answers before you request a place. Nyathi Tours will confirm the final trip details with you.

How do I book a trip?

Send a booking request with your preferred departure and passenger details. Your request is not a confirmed reservation until Nyathi Tours checks availability and accepts it.

What is included in the price?

Inclusions vary by trip and package. Check the trip details, then ask Nyathi Tours to confirm the current inclusions, exclusions and final price before you accept.

When do I pay?

After accepting your request, Nyathi Tours will send an invoice and confirm payment instructions. Online payments are planned for a later phase. Do not send payment until you have confirmed the details directly with Nyathi Tours.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Cancellation and refund terms depend on the trip and supplier. Ask Nyathi Tours for the written terms before accepting the invoice; the confirmed terms should be attached to your booking confirmation.

What travel documents do I need?

Requirements can depend on your citizenship, destination and travel date. Confirm current entry and border requirements with the relevant official authorities before departure.

Can I request a different pickup point or date?

Yes. Include your preferred date and pickup point in the request notes. The owner will confirm whether that route and date are available.
